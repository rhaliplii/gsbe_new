About the project

§ 02

CEM Rădeni is Moldova's largest solar energy complex — 50 MW of installed photovoltaic capacity combined with a 75 MWh BESS grid-scale storage system. Located in Strășeni district, the plant was approved as a national strategic project under Government Decision 83/2025 and financed by MAIB, Moldova's largest bank.

The project was the winning bid at Moldova's first public renewable energy auction (31 March 2025), organized by the Ministry of Energy. GS Blue Electric — as part of the 'Lumina Noastră' Consortium — secured the largest single award at 50 MW installed capacity.

The BESS storage system is part of the same asset: 60 MWh commissioned in March 2026 and extended by 15 MWh in August 2026, to 75 MWh of LiFePO₄ storage (47.5 MW). It provides balancing and ancillary services to the grid. A further 40 MWh extension is being planned.
