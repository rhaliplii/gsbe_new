Moldova's largest BESS, built by Huawei, now inaugurated and commissioned

GS Blue Electric's 60 MWh grid-scale battery storage system in Rădeni has been officially inaugurated and commissioned, making it the largest BESS in Moldova.

Source: ess-news.com ↗

GS Blue Electric has inaugurated and commissioned Moldova's largest battery energy storage system (BESS), a 60 MWh LiFePO₄ grid-scale facility located in Rădeni, Strășeni district. The new facility was backed by private investors as Moldova expands its clean energy ambitions.

The system, equipped with Huawei FusionSolar technology, will provide critical grid stabilisation services and enable more effective integration of the adjacent solar park into Moldova's national energy grid. The BESS became operational in March 2026 and represents a key milestone in Moldova's path towards energy independence and alignment with European energy standards.
