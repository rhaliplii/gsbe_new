GS Blue Electric wins Moldova's first public renewable energy auction

GS Blue Electric was awarded Large Eligible Producer status at the first public auction organized by the Ministry of Energy — a landmark moment for the national energy sector.

Source: gov.md ↗
Official document

Government Decision no. 83/2025

On granting Large Eligible Producer status · Prime Minister Dorin Recean · Minister of Energy Dorin Junghietu

Through Government Decision no. 83/2025, the Government of the Republic of Moldova approved the results of the first public auction for renewable energy and granted Large Eligible Producer (LEP) status to the winning investors — a historic moment for the national energy sector.

GS Blue Electric, as part of the “Lumina Noastră” Consortium, won the auction for 24 MW of solar capacity at the CEM Rădeni plant in Strășeni district — one of the six winning projects in the solar segment. The decision was published in the Official Gazette on 31 March 2025.

LEP status entitles GS Blue Electric to sell the electricity produced at CEM Rădeni to the Central Supplier at a guaranteed fixed price for 15 years from commissioning. The scheme forms part of the state aid programme authorised by Competition Council Decision EMAS-30 of 30 September 2024.

The auction was the first of its kind organised by the Ministry of Energy, with 36 solar and 8 wind bids submitted. Of the 11 winning solar projects, GS Blue Electric — as part of the “Lumina Noastră” Consortium — secured the largest single award at 50 MW installed capacity (24 MW supported), located in Rădeni, Strășeni district.
