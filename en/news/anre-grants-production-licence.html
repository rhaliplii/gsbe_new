Regulatory decision ANRE 10 June 2025

ANRE grants electricity production license to GS Blue Electric

The license for electricity production from renewable energy sources was issued for a period of 25 years.

Source: anre.md ↗
Official document

ANRE Board Decision no. 315 of 10 June 2025

Regarding the issuance of the licence to GS Blue Electric SRL, reg. no. 1022600002267

Through Board Decision no. 315 of 10 June 2025, the National Agency for Energy Regulation (ANRE) issued GS Blue Electric SRL the licence for electricity production from renewable energy sources — valid for a term of 25 years, covering the period 10.06.2025 – 09.06.2050.

The licence was issued in accordance with Law no. 10 of 26.02.2016 on the promotion of energy from renewable sources, Law no. 107 of 27.05.2016 on electricity, and Law no. 160 of 22.07.2011 on the regulation of entrepreneurial activity through authorisation.

With this regulatory decision, GS Blue Electric becomes a licensed electricity producer under Moldovan law, completing the formal licensing framework alongside the Large Eligible Producer status obtained through the Ministry of Energy auction (Government Decision no. 83/2025).
