MAIB continues to support GS Blue Electric by financing Moldova's largest battery energy storage project

MAIB financed the construction of a 60 MWh battery energy storage system in Rădeni, Strășeni. The official inauguration ceremony took place on 30 April 2026.

Source: maib.md ↗

MAIB financed the construction of a 60 MWh battery energy storage system (BESS) in Rădeni, Strășeni district, developed by GS Blue Electric — a long-standing partner of the bank. This is one of the most significant investments in energy storage in the Republic of Moldova to date. The official inauguration ceremony took place on 30 April 2026.

The facility, located adjacent to the 50 MW Rădeni Solar Park, is the largest grid-connected storage system in the Republic of Moldova and one of the largest in the region. The project will provide essential balancing services to the national grid operator Moldelectrica, and will substantially increase the share of solar energy that can be reliably integrated into the national energy system.

Preliminary estimates indicate the project will reduce emissions by approximately 7,000–9,000 tonnes of CO₂ equivalent per year and will reduce Moldova's net dependence on electricity imports by an estimated 0.6–0.7%.

“MAIB has offered consistent support to our group of companies, and the partnership we have developed with MAIB has allowed us to turn ambitious projects into reality.” Valerian Stratan, GS Blue Electric
“The BESS system in Rădeni is a landmark project both in terms of its scale and in demonstrating that private investment in energy storage is viable and attractive for financing in our region.” Alexandru Sonic, Vice President maib, Corporate Division

This project consolidates maib's position as a leader in green financing in Moldova and demonstrates the bank's commitment to supporting infrastructure that grows the country's energy security and accelerates the transition to renewable energy.
