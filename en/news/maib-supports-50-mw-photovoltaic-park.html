MAIB supports the inauguration of a 50 MW photovoltaic park in Moldova

GS Blue Electric inaugurated one of the largest photovoltaic parks in Moldova, supported by MAIB through a green loan.

Source: ir.maib.md ↗

GS Blue Electric has inaugurated one of the largest photovoltaic parks in Moldova, located in Rădeni, Strășeni district. The solar park, with a total installed capacity of 50 MW, represents a significant milestone in Moldova's efforts to increase its energy independence. MAIB supported this initiative through a green loan over a period of 8 years.

A step forward in sustainable lending

The project aligns with MAIB's sustainability strategy. The bank has set a target of a 17% share of green loans by 2027 and 23% by 2030, already surpassing this benchmark in its corporate portfolio. As of the end of Q1 2025, MAIB's total green portfolio exceeded MDL 1.2 billion.

International recognition

MAIB received the “Green Trade Deal of the Year” award from the EBRD, as part of the 2024 Trade Facilitation Programme awards.

“We are proud to finance such an ambitious project. Green energy is no longer a choice, it is a necessity.” Giorgi Shagidze, CEO MAIB
“The launch of this photovoltaic power plant marks a key milestone for the development of green infrastructure in Moldova.” Liudmila Vîntu, Administrator GS Blue Electric
