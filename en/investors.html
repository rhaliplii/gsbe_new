Grid context§ 04

ENTSO-E integration and export corridors

Moldova's power system is synchronised with ENTSO-E via Romania and Ukraine. Three 400 kV interconnections expand transfer capacity and open regional market access for merchant volumes.

Vulcănești – Isaccea
400 kV · Moldova – Romania
The main import corridor from the ENTSO-E system, normally carrying 60–70% of consumption on the right bank of the Dniester.
Bălți – Suceava
400 kV · 48 km · 12 localities
Completion planned 2027
Total cost EUR 77 million — a EUR 15.4 million EU grant, a EUR 30.8 million EBRD loan and EUR 30.8 million from the European Investment Bank. It will raise import and export capacity and allow more renewable generation to be integrated.
Strășeni – Gutinaș (Liberty Line)
400 kV · ~70 km in Moldova
Feasibility study completed · June 2026
The third high-voltage interconnection with the European system through Romania, financed by a USD 130 million grant from the U.S. Government. The route crosses 25 localities including Strășeni district, where CEM Rădeni is located.

Sources: gov.md — Liberty Line feasibility study (18.06.2026) · Ministry of Energy — EBRD tender, Bălți–Suceava (31.07.2025). Infrastructure operated by Moldelectrica; information current as at August 2026.
