Why GS Blue Electric

§ 01
Scale

Projects of national scale

CEM Rădeni is Moldova's largest solar park, with the country's largest BESS storage system — a national strategic project under Government Decision 83/2025.

Technology

State-of-the-art equipment

Work with Jinko Solar Tiger Neo panels, Huawei FusionSolar inverters, LiFePO₄ storage and a 110 kV grid connection.

Growth

A local team, growing fast

A 100% locally owned company with an active pipeline: wind parks, storage expansion and flexible gas generation.
