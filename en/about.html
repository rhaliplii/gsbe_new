Our story§ 01

Regenerating energy. Reinventing the future.

GS Blue Electric was incorporated in 2024 with a clear mandate: to develop, own, and operate long-life energy infrastructure assets in Moldova, structured to institutional standards from day one.

We are a long-term infrastructure platform — owning, operating, and expanding our asset base through structured financing, partnership with Moldova's largest bank, and government-approved project status.
