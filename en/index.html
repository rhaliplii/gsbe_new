Platform overview§ 01

Moldova's first integrated energy-infrastructure platform, built to institutional standards with local capital.

Founded in 2024, GS Blue Electric was structured from the outset as a long-term energy infrastructure platform — not a project developer, but a strategic asset operator built to European standards.

Our flagship asset — the CEM Rădeni solar and BESS complex in Strășeni — is approved as a national strategic project by Government Decision 83/2025 and financed by MAIB, Moldova's largest bank.
