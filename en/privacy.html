Privacy & cookie policy

How we process the personal data of visitors to gselectric.md.

This policy explains how GS Blue Electric SRL processes the personal data of visitors to gselectric.md, and which cookies are used.

Data controller

GS Blue Electric SRL, registration number 1024600034565, registered at str. Mitropolit Gavriil Bănulescu-Bodoni 57/1, office 402, MD-2005 Chișinău, Republic of Moldova. For any request concerning personal data: contact@gselectric.md.

What we collect

This is an informational website. It contains no contact forms, user accounts, shopping baskets or advertising trackers. We do not use Google Analytics or any other traffic analytics platform.

  • Server log data: IP address, browser type, page requested and time of access — recorded automatically by our hosting provider for security and technical diagnostics.
  • Correspondence: if you write to the published email address, we process the data you include in your message solely in order to reply.

Cookies

The site sets no analytics, advertising or profiling cookies. No cookie requiring consent is placed on your first visit.

Third-party services

Typefaces are hosted on our own servers, so rendering a page generates no requests to external services.

The Contact and CEM Rădeni pages include a Google Maps embed. The map does not load automatically: third-party content is requested only after you press the “Load map” button. From that point, Google LLC may set cookies and process your IP address in accordance with its own privacy policy.

News pages link to external sources (maib.md, anre.md, gov.md, ess-news.com). The privacy policies of those sites apply once you leave gselectric.md.

Legal basis and retention

Log data is processed on the basis of our legitimate interest in maintaining the security and availability of the site, and is retained for a limited period necessary for that purpose. Correspondence is retained for as long as needed to handle the request and to meet statutory archiving obligations.

Your rights

Under Law no. 133/2011 on personal data protection and, where applicable, Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR), you have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, objection and data portability.

Requests should be sent to contact@gselectric.md. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the National Centre for Personal Data Protection of the Republic of Moldova.

Updates

This policy may be updated. The version in force is the one published on this page.
